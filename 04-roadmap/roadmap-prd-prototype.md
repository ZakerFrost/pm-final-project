# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** A low-intent subscriber who wants easy entertainment but rarely invests effort discovering something new.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** New-title Start Rate from Spotlight among Wanderers/Casual Browsers.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** Discovery requires too much effort, causing users to default to familiar or trending content instead of exploring.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Power User sessions/week stays within 5% of the 4.8/week baseline.

## Scan the backlog & set a human baseline
- **My instinctive “quick wins” before touching the AI (2 to 3 feature IDs + why):** A1, since it matches the original problem. First row content is already low-intent oriented.
A4, since it feels oriented to the same low-energy users who don't feel like spending time scrolling through the catalog. Curated list towards a specific mood should improve that.
A6, even before the user has opened the app, there will be data sent to them, which may trigger some of the Wanderers and Casual Viewers to actually open it and go to those titles.

## Audit, override & decide
- **Where did you override the AI? (feature + old vs. new score + why):** A2, I think it has less value, AI said 3, I think it's a 2 at most.
- **Did the AI over-value a Sales/Eng request your M2 interviews don’t support?:** No, thankfully.
- **Did it underweight something your M3 cohort/funnel data strongly supports?:** A6, but because it doesn't move my primary metric, it instead brings back users externally without improving the in-app discovery.

## Generate your interactive roadmap
- **My “Now” lane (this sprint), the 2 to 3 quick wins I’ll build first:** - A1 Spotlight Curated Rail, Directly shortens discovery and exposes new titles without asking low-intent users to search.
- **What I cut, and the “no” I’m protecting the scope from:** - A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label, Explains recommendations after discovery but adds AI complexity without substantially improving title relevance.
- A6 Spotlight Digest Email, Can return Wanderers with viewing intent, but indirectly attacks in-product discovery and needs personalization tooling.
- A7 Curator Profiles, Requires users to actively manage curator preferences—the opposite of effortless discovery.
- A8 Watch Party (Spotlight), Solves social viewing after selection and has virtually no connection to the discovery bottleneck.
- A9 Advanced Filter Engine, Adds sophisticated browsing controls to a persona whose core problem is unwillingness to browse.
- A10 Offline Download (Spotlight), Improves viewing after selection but cannot move the Spotlight discovery-to-start problem.
