## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** “I just re-watch the same four comfort shows. Discovering anything new feels like a chore, so I don't.” - Aisha
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** Wanderers are 41% of the base but average only 1.1 sessions/week, and Spotlight-exposed Wanderers show a 22-point churn improvement versus non-exposed users. Spotlight cohorts retain 12–15 points better at Month 1 than Full Library cohorts.
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** A low-intent subscriber who wants easy entertainment but rarely invests much effort discovering something new
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Discovering something new requires too much effort, so low-intent users default to familiar or trending content instead of exploring the catalogue.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** Increase successful discovery and new-content starts among Wanderers and Casual Browsers, leading to higher repeat engagement, improved retention, and lower churn.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** % of targeted Spotlight-exposed sessions resulting in playback of a title the user has not previously watched.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Power User weekly sessions remain within 5% of the existing 4.8 sessions/week baseline.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** 4–6 weeks for Month-1 retention validation. Scale at approximately +3pp new-title starts and +5pp Month-1 retention
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** Based on Aisha’s feedback that discovering new content “feels like a chore,” and data showing Wanderers represent 41% of users and improve churn by 22 points when Spotlight-exposed, I believe that reducing discovery effort through a small, relevant, human-curated selection for low-intent Casual Browsers and Wanderers will result in more successful discovery and stronger repeat engagement, as measured by a 10% increase in new-title Start Rate versus control. I will protect Power User sessions/week, allowing no more than a 5% decline from the 4.8/week baseline, and make a go/no-go decision after 4–6 weeks, once Month-1 retention can also be evaluated.
