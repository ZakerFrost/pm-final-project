Data-backed hypothesis

Based on Aisha’s feedback that discovering new content “feels like a chore,” and data showing Wanderers represent 41% of users and improve churn by 22 points when Spotlight-exposed, I believe that reducing discovery effort through a small, relevant, human-curated selection for low-intent Casual Browsers and Wanderers will result in more successful discovery and stronger repeat engagement, as measured by a 10% increase in new-title Start Rate versus control. I will protect Power User sessions/week, allowing no more than a 5% decline from the 4.8/week baseline, and make a go/no-go decision after 4–6 weeks, once Month-1 retention can also be evaluated.

PRD highlights
The system must display one Spotlight Curated Rail within the first homepage viewport.
The system must display between 5 and 7 human-curated titles in the rail.
The system must visually distinguish Spotlight from algorithmic recommendations using editorial labeling.
The system must allow users to select any Spotlight title with one interaction from the homepage.
The system must allow users to start playback within one additional interaction after title selection.
The system must record prototype events for Spotlight impression, title selection, and new-title start.
The system must preserve the existing homepage experience outside Spotlight to avoid disrupting Power User behavior.
Blueprint · Ruthless scope for a 2 engineer + 1 designer team
08 · Validation

Experimentation Plan

50 / 50 · 14 DAYS · p < 0.05
Hypothesis

I believe that the Spotlight Curated Rail for low-intent Wanderers and Casual Browsers who want easy entertainment but rarely invest effort discovering something new will result in more successful transitions from homepage discovery to playback, as measured by a +3 percentage-point absolute increase in Start Rate, from 29% to 32%, within 14 days. We will protect Power User weekly sessions, ensuring they remain within 5% of the 4.8 sessions/week baseline throughout the test.

Control A

Low-intent users use the existing StreamLine homepage and recommendation experience. Discovery often requires prolonged browsing, causing some users to return to familiar comfort shows or leave without starting anything new; current Start Playing rate is approximately 29%.

Variant B

Add one human-curated Spotlight rail to the homepage.

Primary metric

Start Rate = % of eligible Wanderer/Casual Browser homepage sessions resulting in playback starting.

10 · Story

From Vocal Minority to Signal

FRICTION → AHA → NEXT
Friction + aha moment

After M2 the AI gave me a couple personas, one of them were the vocal minority from the feedback, mostly wanted synch in between devices. Once M3 arrived with the data, it became no longer relevant, but finally saw clearly what the issue really was. Not the vocal minority but rather the silence of the numbers shown in the different metrics.

Key takeaway

Data / Metrics are so important to not be lead by the vocal minority, be that customer feedback, salesmen, or other internal stakeholders.
