# GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard (Module 6)

## Get your prior work ready
- **The feature you're launching from your M4 PRD, one feature, not a list:** Spotlight Curated Rail
- **What your M5 experiment told you shipped / iterating, the evidence behind the launch:** Whether this feature increases the % of play-rate in between the set of Casual Users and Wanderers.
- **Your persona pull your M2 persona, this anchors your audience:** A low-intent subscriber who wants easy entertainment but rarely invests effort discovering something new.

## Set your goal, then your audience
- **Primary GTM goal awareness · engagement · conversion, pick one:** Engagement
- **Why this goal? what makes this the right goal for this feature right now:** Increase playback engagement among existing low-intent subscribers by reducing the effort required to choose something to watch.
- **Target audience the specific segment your goal implies, be precise:** Existing Wanderers and Casual Browsers with low discovery-to-play conversion.

## Size your launch
- **Launch tier S (minimal) · M (targeted) · L (multi-channel) · XL (full GTM):** M
- **Justification reach + revenue impact + what silence would risk:** Spotlight changes the discovery experience for a defined segment of the existing subscriber base and is intended to improve engagement and retention, but does not change pricing, packaging, acquisition strategy, or the core StreamLine proposition. No paid campaign or broad-market launch is required.
- **Is this a launch or a release? does it need go-to-market, or can it just ship?:** Launch, we are trying to create awareness even in between those who may not log in to see the changes

## Choose your top three channels and plan assets
- **Channel 1 owned / earned / paid, and why it reaches your audience:** Owned. Targeted emails to the segment of users if we can identify Wanderers and Casual Browsers
- **Channel 2 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Owned. Push notifications targeted to low recent engagement, eligible user segments of Wanderers and casuals and have the push notifications enabled, excluding already-high-frequency viewers
- **Channel 3 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Owned. In-app messaging that directs the attention to the rail when inside the app for the user segments that apply.
- **Enablement & assets what Sales / CS / Support need, plus the assets to build (one-pager, demo, etc.):** Internal one-pager: audience, problem, positioning, feature behavior, metrics.
Messaging brief: one core value proposition + approved email/push/in-app variants.
Product screenshots/GIF: showing Spotlight location and interaction.
FAQ / What's changing: explicitly state what Spotlight does and does not change.
Lifecycle assets: email subject/body, push copy, deep link.
In-app launch asset: tooltip/banner/announcement copy.
Measurement dashboard: Spotlight exposure → click → playback.

- **Budget & resource gaps what costs extra, and any asset you can't currently build:** No incremental paid-media budget; launch relies on existing Product, Design, Engineering and Lifecycle capacity.
- **Timeline Phase 1 beta → Phase 2 launch moment → Phase 3 post-launch:** Early Access — Validate: Verify that users understand Spotlight as an easier discovery path and that messaging drives qualified interaction.
Launch — Activate: Maximum qualified Spotlight exposure among the target audience.
Post-Launch — Sustain: Continue curated refreshes and selectively re-engage low-intent users who have not interacted with Spotlight

## Define how you'll know it worked
- **Success metrics 2 to 3 metrics that match your GTM goal:** % of eligible homepage sessions resulting in playback.
% of Spotlight-exposed users selecting a Spotlight title.
% of users selecting a Spotlight title who then start playback.
% of targeted push/email recipients who reach Spotlight and subsequently start playback.
- **Bad signal to watch for e.g. high reach, zero signups = message-market mismatch:** High Spotlight exposure but low title interaction or playback conversion.
- **Most likely post-launch decision double-down · iterate · pivot · deprioritize, and what would trigger it:** DOUBLE-DOWN if Start Rate reaches ≥32% without breaching the Power User guardrail; expand exposure and invest in Spotlight refresh/iteration. 
DEPRIORITIZE if Start Rate remains ≤29% after the agreed evaluation window and sufficient exposure, unless instrumentation or launch execution invalidated the read.
