# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A frequent subscriber who opens the service wanting something new but relies heavily on recommendations to decide what to watch.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Find a compelling title quickly without having to evaluate hundreds of options.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** They scroll through recommendation rows for an extended period, find nothing that feels relevant, and leave without watching anything.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Physical media / DVD or competing streaming or entertainment services
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Enter the app intending to watch something.
2. Browse recommendations for an extended period.
3. Become frustrated by the lack of compelling choices.
4. End the session without starting anything.
5. Switch to an alternative source of entertainment.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The user may already be paying for the service and have access to thousands of titles, but still has to use another source because the platform cannot convert catalogue breadth into a confident viewing decision.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** UXR-01 · Priya, 34, heavy viewer (14 yrs) "I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything. There's 15,000 titles but nothing I actually want. I ended up going back to a DVD."
