# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A subscriber who discovers, saves, and watches content across their phone, tablet, and television.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** They want their watchlist and playback progress to follow them seamlessly between devices.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Their “Moment of Misery” occurs when a saved title disappears or a partially watched programme restarts from the beginning on another device, causing them to abandon content they had already chosen.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Completely manual process, having access to the device where they have the saved title and/or watching progress, or changing platforms.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Remember the missing title and search for it again.
2. Browse the catalogue to reconstruct where they originally found it.
3. Estimate their previous playback position from memory.
4. Scrub through the video until they recognize the last scene watched.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** A title saved on mobile is absent when the user opens the television app.
A title paused partway through on a tablet restarts from the beginning on the television.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** “I added a film to My List on my phone on the train. Got home, opened the TV app, gone. Not there. So I never found it again.” - Ken, UXR-06
