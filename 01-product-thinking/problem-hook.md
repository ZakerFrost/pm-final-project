# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months the company will keep losing engagement, reputation will consolidate and competitors will gain advantage.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Going to competitors like Netflix, Prime, HBO, Crunchyroll, or even Pirate sites.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must solve our low growth in engagement by addressing the quality of our recommended content.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For the users, we will improve the recommendations of the catalog because they cannot seem to find content tailored to their tastes.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** It is high enough to justify taking an initiative to move the needle in the direction of retaining customers.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** A systemic one, the platform is not curating the titles and giving recommendations based on user-data.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** It is oriented to remove the obstacle, which is the recommendations and user-retention, by pointing towards content that will be used by the specific users. Thus keeping them happy with what they get.
