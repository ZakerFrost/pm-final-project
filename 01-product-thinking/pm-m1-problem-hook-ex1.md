# Strategic Product Thinking, Module 1 Lab (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **Write one feature from the map that, if an engineer asked “why is this a priority for our 12-week roadmap?”, you honestly could not give a strategic answer for.:** Containers (favourite bottles/cups, custom sizes and tracking by % of bottle)
- **Write one feature that looks “correct” for the app but has zero strategic weight, you have no idea if it helps the company make money or whether a single user actually asked for it.:** Personalized insights and tips
- **Compare V3 to your Step 1 baseline. What changed, and is the output objectively better, did the AI get smarter, or did your added PM knowledge (JTBD, effort/impact, retention) force it to be useful?:** Way better and more structured, the prompt forced the AI to filter the features through a different lens and give a much more useful result
- **Key takeaway: how does this prove the PM’s role is to think while the AI’s role is to execute?:** We think what we need for ourselves and what we also need to communicate, the whats and the whys. We put all of that into a prompt and then the AI executes our idea. We then think if what we received was the optimal result or we need to iterate on our prompt.
